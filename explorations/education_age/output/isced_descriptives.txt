================================================================
  CODING SCHEME COMPARISON
================================================================

ISCED 1997 (W4 standalone, W5 ef1_isce, y15_ISCED_lt W4-W6):
  Code  Label
  0     Pre-primary education
  1     Primary education (ISCED 1)
  2     Lower secondary education (ISCED 2)
  3     Upper secondary education (ISCED 3)
  4     Post-secondary non-tertiary (ISCED 4)
  5     First stage of tertiary (ISCED 5)
  6     Second stage of tertiary (ISCED 6)

ISCED 2011 (W6/W8 via 91-24 trend):
  Code  Label
  0     Early childhood / less than primary
  1     Primary education
  2     Lower secondary education
  3     Upper secondary education
  4     Post-secondary non-tertiary education
  5     Short-cycle tertiary education
  6     Bachelor's or equivalent
  7     Master's or equivalent
  8     Doctoral or equivalent

Crosswalk ISCED 1997 -> ISCED 2011:
  1997 codes 0-4 -> 2011 codes 0-4 (direct mapping)
  1997 code 5    -> 2011 codes 5 or 6 (split: short-cycle vs bachelor)
  1997 code 6    -> 2011 codes 7 or 8 (split: master vs doctoral)
  Implication: Harmonizing to 3 categories (low/mid/high) avoids this split.


================================================================
  HARMONIZATION NOTES
================================================================

1. NO ISCED FOR W1-W3/W3cc: The isced column exists in standalone files
   but is empty. No ISCED classification was derived for these early waves.
   The ef1 column (raw education question) is also empty in these files.

2. W4-W5 (2005-2010): ISCED 1997 coding (0-6). Available from standalone
   files and from y15_ISCED_lt in the 91-15 trend (harmonized W4-W6).

3. W6-W8 (2015-2024): ISCED 2011 coding (0-8). Available from the 91-24
   trend file. Also have pre-computed edu3/edu4/edu5 recodes.

4. SIMPLEST HARMONIZATION: 3 categories (low/mid/high)
     Low  = ISCED 0-2 (primary or less)
     Mid  = ISCED 3-4 (secondary)
     High = ISCED 5+  (tertiary)
   Works for W4-W5 (ISCED 0-2/3-4/5-6) and W6-W8 (ISCED 0-2/3-4/5-8).
   NOT available for W1-W3/W3cc.

5. COMBINED EDUCATION VARIABLE COVERAGE:

   Wave          educ-age (q2b/q5)    ISCED        Strategy
   ---------------------------------------------------------------
   W1 (1991)     YES (censored)       NO           Use q2b directly
   W2 (1995)     YES (3-cat only)     NO           Coarse assignment
   W3 (2000)     NO                   NO           Cannot assign
   W3cc (2001)   YES (actual ages)    NO           Use q2b directly
   W4 (2005)     YES (actual ages)    YES (0-6)    Both available
   W5 (2010)     YES (actual ages)    YES (0-6)    Both available
   W6 (2015)     NO                   YES (0-8)    ISCED + typical ages
   W8 (2024)     NO                   YES (0-8)    ISCED + typical ages

6. FOR LABOR MARKET ENTRY TIMING:
   - Where q2b/q5 available: entry_year ~ survey_year - (age - educ_age)
   - Where only ISCED available: need typical graduation ages by ISCED
     level and country to approximate entry year
   - W3 main wave: no education data at all — cannot assign entry timing
